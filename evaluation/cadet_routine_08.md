Hello Jim,
My routine in the Academy starts very early in the morning. Every day, I wake up at 06:00 am. I take a fast shower, then I put on my army combat uniform and go to the Alpatacal Courtyard for line up with my comrades. We have to go to breakfast in the mess hall at about 06:40 am. After that, I have classes from 8 am until 1 pm. I'm very tired during the day but later I have lunch and athletics. I love it so much. After that, in the afternoon we go to the mess hall again for dinner. Finally, I have to go to study one more time until the night. Later we have to form up in the formation are and listen the instructions of my lieutenant or brigadier and go to sleep in the barracks. Tomorrow is a new day again.
