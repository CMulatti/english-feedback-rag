## Teacher justification
- Content: Covers the whole daily routine with interesting details, such as what he eats for breakfast and his physical training. Free time is only suggested (playing basketball, calling his dad) and he doesn't say how often he does these activities, so the reader is not fully informed about those points. The text is about 100 words, within the expected length.
- Organization: The text follows the order of the day and uses a range of connectors ("Next", "After that", "Then", "Also", "Finally"), which on its own would correspond to band 4, although "Next" and "After that" are repeated several times. However, some sentences lack punctuation or a simple connector ("I have to shower Then", "I call my dad I go sleep"), which makes the text sound choppy and harder to follow. This lowers the score to 3.5: the text fully
meets band 3 and shows some features of the bands above, but not consistently enough for band 4. There is no greeting, so it does not follow the format of a reply to Jim, which lowers the score by half a band: 3.
- Language: Uses present simple consistently, with subjects in almost every sentence, and uses the adverb of frequency "usually" correctly. Includes taught vocabulary (army combat uniform, form up, mess hall, barracks, gym).
However, there is Spanish interference at the word level ("ingles","basketbol"), spelling mistakes in key words ("traing", "dificult", "coffe") and some errors in basic structures ("get dressed my army combat uniform", "I go sleep"). Meaning can always be understood, but the errors are frequent enough to show only some degree of control, which corresponds to band 3.
