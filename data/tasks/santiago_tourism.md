# TASK: santiago_tourism
Level: A2+ - Second year - Course 1
Instructions: Do you see a lot of tourists in Santiago? Do you help them if they're lost?
Is Santiago a good city for tourists? Why / Why not?
Describe Santiago and compare it with another city that you can recommend to tourists.

## Expected length
Minimum words: 120
Maximum words: 150

## Expected format
No specific format. A paragraph is enough.

## Required content points
1. [accessory] Whether they see tourists in Santiago and if they help them when they're lost
2. [essential] Opinion: is Santiago a good city for tourists, with reasons
3. [essential] Description of Santiago (it can be included within the comparison)
4. [essential] Comparison between Santiago and another city
5. [essential] Recommendation of the other city to tourists

## Suggested ideas (not required)
Tourist attractions, food, people, cost of things, weather, safety.

## Target grammar
- Comparatives and superlatives
- (not) as ... as

## Taught vocabulary
- Adjectives: large, small, exciting, boring, quiet, stressful, peaceful,
  dirty, clean, cold, warm, dangerous, safe, crowded
- Tourist activities: visit the museums, visit the art galleries, try the
  local food, buy souvenirs, take photographs, take pictures, go shopping,
  visit the famous landmarks, eat in a fancy restaurant, go on a sightseeing
  tour, visit the local markets, relax, go to the beach, spend time in nature,
  go hiking, go to the amusement park, ride a rollercoaster.
